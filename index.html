File Transfer for Android

Transfer files from Android to Mac. Copy photos, videos and files between your Android phone and your Mac over a USB cable. Nothing to install on the phone.

Questions, problems or ideas: mailmuneef@gmail.com. Tell us your Mac's macOS version and your phone's model, and what you saw.

Getting started

  1. Connect your Android phone to your Mac with a USB cable that carries data (some charging-only cables don't).
  2. Unlock the phone and tap Allow when it asks to allow access to phone data.
  3. Browse the phone in the app and copy what you need, or drag files to Finder.

If your phone doesn't show up

  • Choose File transfer on the phone. Unlock it, swipe down from the top of the screen, tap the USB notification, and choose File transfer (some phones call it Transferring files or MTP).
  • Closed the Allow message by mistake? Click Show Prompt Again in the app, then tap Allow on the phone.
  • Another app is using the phone. Photos, Image Capture, Preview and other phone tools can hold the phone's connection. The app tells you which one; quit it, then unplug the phone and plug it in again.
  • Try another cable or port, preferably one directly on the Mac rather than through a hub.

Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple silicon or Intel, running macOS Sonoma 14 or later.
  • An Android phone or tablet that offers File transfer over USB, as most do.

Privacy

The app collects no data. Your files go straight over the cable from your phone to your Mac. Read the privacy policy.
