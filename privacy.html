File Transfer for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

File Transfer for Android is a Mac app made by Kolice LLC that copies photos, videos and files between an Android phone and a Mac over a USB cable. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that does any of these.

Your files

Your files go straight over the cable between your phone and your Mac. They are never uploaded anywhere, and we never see them. The app does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your Mac

To show your phone quickly the next time you connect it, the app keeps a few things on your Mac: the names of phones you've connected, a list of the folders and file names on them, small previews of photos, and your settings (such as the folder you last copied to). This information never leaves your Mac. To remove it, quit the app, then delete the app's folders in your home folder's Library › Containers and Library › Group Containers (their names contain "androidfiletransfer").

Permissions

The app asks macOS for access to USB devices so it can talk to your phone, and for access to the folders you choose so it can save copies there. It uses them for nothing else.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
